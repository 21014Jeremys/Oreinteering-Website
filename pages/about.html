What is Orienteering?

Orienteering is a sport were the goal is to navagate to checkpoints in the quickest most efficent way. There are two main types of orienteering: Set Course and Rogain

Set Course:

Set course is genrally what is refered to when someone talks about orienteering. With a set course you are given a map with controls listed in an order, and you have to get to all of them in the correct order, and in the shortest period of time. There are genrally four difficultys of course: White being easyest, then yellow, orange and red at the hardest.

Rogain:

Rogains are different to set courses, becaus all controls are optional. This makes it less servear than set cources as missing a control isn't a big deal. Rather then being given a course, you are given a map with lots of scattered controls (usally 30) and your goal is to get as many as possable in the given time limit (usally 1 hour). If two or more people tie on points, the person with the shortest time wins.
