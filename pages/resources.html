Permanent Course

OBOP (Orienteering Bay Of Plenty) has a permanent course set up in the Redwoods forest. This course is free to use for casual users. Rather than finding flags, insted you are looking for orange and white plastic squares, as showen in image on right ⟶
Each Checkpoint has a unique two letter combination to prove you have been there rather than clippers or Sport-ident.
